\# 🇮🇳 NagrikSetu



\## Explainable Government Benefit Navigation Platform



NagrikSetu is a citizen-centric platform that helps people discover government welfare schemes they may be eligible for.



Instead of simply listing schemes, NagrikSetu analyzes a citizen's profile, evaluates eligibility, explains why a scheme is suitable or unsuitable, identifies missing information/documents, and provides access to official application sources.

\## 🎯 Problem Statement



Government welfare schemes are often difficult for citizens to discover and understand because:



\- There are many schemes across different departments.

\- Eligibility conditions can be complicated.

\- Citizens may not know which schemes apply to them.

\- Required documents are not always clear.

\- Citizens may not understand why they qualify or do not qualify.

\- Information is distributed across different government sources.



NagrikSetu addresses this problem by providing a simple and explainable interface for scheme discovery and eligibility evaluation.
